About

Local, licensed and focused on safety.

Greenroom Electrical is run by Grant Walker, a licensed electrician based in the Tweed Heads / Gold Coast area. Greenroom provides residential electrical services: LED conversions, ceiling fans, switches and powerpoints, test and tag, smart lighting, smoke alarms, safety switch testing and switchboard compliance checks.
